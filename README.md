# Job Apply Bot

Free local app that finds jobs on LinkedIn and Indeed, matches them to your resume, and applies for you. Nothing is sent to a paid service. The AI model runs on your computer.

## Download

Open the [latest release](https://github.com/AreebMughal/job-apply-bot/releases/latest) and download the file for your computer.

| Computer | File |
| --- | --- |
| Windows 10/11 (64-bit) | `JobApplyBot-windows.zip` |
| macOS on Apple Silicon (M1–M4) | `JobApplyBot-macos.tar.gz` |
| Linux x64 (Ubuntu 22.04 or newer) | `JobApplyBot-linux.tar.gz` |

Unpack it and read **START HERE.txt** inside.

The first time you open the app, go to **AI model** and download a model (about 2 GB). Then open **Settings** and save your LinkedIn and Indeed login. Those stay on this computer.
